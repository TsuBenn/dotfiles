Welcome to **Quick Menu**! This utility allows you to instantly trigger actions using custom keybinds.

When activated, a popup appears that disables almost every desktop keyboard bind, giving you total control over every possible key mapped to *any action*.

## INTERFACE OVERVIEW
The popup displays a list of mapped actions. Each item includes:
* An action **dropdown menu**
* A **text field** to type your keybind

Below the list, you will find:
* An `Add` button to create new mappings
* A `Manage custom actions` button

To trigger the listed actions, you can simply press the keybind on your keyboard of the corresponding action and it will trigger it.

## KEYBIND RULES
When typing binds into the text field, please follow these formatting rules:

### 1. Modifiers & Syntax
* Modifiers can be written as the first letter, an abbreviation, or the full word.
  > *Example:* `c`, `ctrl`, or `control`

* Modifiers must join the final key using either a `+` or a `-`.
  > *Example:* `Control+C` or `C-c`

### 2. Forbidden Keys
* The `Meta`/`Super` key and `Escape` are strictly forbidden.
* These are desktop-exclusive keys.

### 3. Duplicates
* Duplicate binds are not allowed.
* The *first* bind is kept.
* The duplicate is ignored and marked with an exclamation mark (`!`).

### 4. Supported Keys
* System:
  - `ret` or `return`
  - `bs` or `backspace`
  - `tab`
  - `end`

* Navigation:
  - `up`
  - `down`
  - `left`
  - `right`
  - `pup` or `pageup`
  - `pdown` or `pagedown`

* Alphanumeric:
  - `A` to `Z`
  - `0` to `9`

* Function:
  - `F1` to `F35`

* Brackets:
  - All open/closed types

* Symbols:
  - `tick` or `
  - `plus` or `+`
  - `minus` or `-`
  - `equal` or `=`
  - `slash` or `/`
  - `backslash` or `\`
  - `quote` or `"`
  - `singlequote`, `apostrophe` or `'`
  - `pipe` or `|`
  - `dot`, `period` or `.`
  - `comma` or `,`
  - `exclamation` or `!`
  - `at` or `@`
  - `hashtag`, `tag` or `#`
  - `dollar` or `$`
  - `percent` or `%`
  - `caret`, `hat` or `^`
  - `amp`, `and` or `&`
  - `star`, `multiply` or `*`
  - `tilde` or `~`

### 5. Unsupported Keys
* Unrecognized keys will trigger an error.
* They will be colored **red**, marked with `!`, or simply ignored.

## Actions & Customization
The action dropdown defaults to built-in desktop actions. You can expand this by adding your own.

### Managing Custom Actions
* Click `Manage custom actions` to begin.
* Each custom action requires:
  * A **label**
  * A **shell command**

* Commands execute in a single line. For complex tasks, call a script or binary:
  > `sh -c path/to/your/script`

* Custom actions can be added or removed at any time.

### Overriding & Orphaned Binds
* Custom labels **override** default actions if they share a similar name.
* Labels are converted to `snake_case` as their ID for internal lookup.
* If a keybind is mapped to an action that gets removed, it automatically defaults to `No operation`.
